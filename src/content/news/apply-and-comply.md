LSTA encourages operators to check that their vehicles and operating arrangements meet the requirements that apply to them. The announcement highlights permits, roadworthiness, driver documentation, passenger loads and agreements with parents.

For current legal requirements, operators should confirm the details with the relevant authorities and their professional advisers.
