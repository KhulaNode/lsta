A reliable school journey depends on good communication between everyone involved. Operators can make their own pre-trip routine visible to families and schools, and agree on a clear way to report delays or changes.

## Questions worth discussing

- Who should parents contact if a vehicle is late?
- How will collection and drop-off changes be shared?
- What is the agreed process when a learner is absent?

These prompts are general information. Operators should confirm current legal requirements with the relevant authorities and their professional advisers.
